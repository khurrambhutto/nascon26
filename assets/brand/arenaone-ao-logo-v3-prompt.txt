Use case: logo-brand. Design ONE refined original logo for ArenaOne, a multi-sport league and club platform. The user specifically wants a clear combination of uppercase A and uppercase O, tied to a sports arena. Primary concept: a bold custom capital A interlocked with a complete capital O stadium ring. Both A and O MUST remain immediately readable: a strong triangular A with clear counter and crossbar, nested across an oval O whose two restrained inner curved lines suggest a stadium running track. One integrated compact monogram, not two unrelated letters beside each other. Aim for sophisticated professional sports identity, precise geometric construction, confident balanced proportions, distinctive generous negative space, clean enough to recognize at 32 pixels. Palette: main deep forest teal #173F3A, a single terracotta coral #CF563E highlight on the A crossbar or short arena segment. Flat opaque solid shapes, no gradients, texture, 3D, shadows, shading, tiny details or hairlines. Square composition, one centered isolated logo mark, generous transparent margin. No wordmark, no additional letters, no numeral 1, no slogan, no shield, no animals, no trophy, no ball, no device, no app tile or presentation sheet, no watermark. Do not make a generic swoosh or abstract arrow: this must clearly read as the letters A and O fused with a stadium theme. Actually transparent background.
